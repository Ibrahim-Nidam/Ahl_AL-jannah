# Privacy Policy — Ahl Jannah

*Last updated: August 3, 2026*

Thank you for using **Ahl Jannah** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We respect your privacy: the App has **no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising**, and your data is never sold.

## 1. Data we collect

The App collects the minimum data needed to provide its core features:

- **Precise location** — collected via GPS (through the `geolocator` permission) to compute accurate **prayer times** for your location and to determine the direction of the **Qibla** (Kaaba).
- **Approximate location** — when GPS is unavailable or permission is denied, the App falls back to IP-based geolocation (city-level) to estimate your region for prayer times.

No other personal data (name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or device identifiers) is collected or required.

## 2. Permissions we use

- **Location (approximate / precise)** — used only for prayer times and Qibla direction. You can deny this permission and the App will let you set your city manually.
- **Notifications** — used to send prayer-time reminders that you choose to enable.
- **Exact alarm** — used to schedule prayer reminders on time when your device permits.

## 3. How data is used and shared

Location data is used **solely for app functionality** (prayer times and Qibla). To compute outcomes we may send location data to the following third-party services **over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)**:

- **Aladhan** (`api.aladhan.com`) — to compute prayer times.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** (`nominatim.openstreetmap.org`) — to determine your city name from coordinates.
- **IP location services** (`ipapi.co`, `ip-api.com`) — as a fallback for approximate location when GPS is unavailable.

We do **not** use location, or any other data, for advertising, marketing, or analytics, and we do not sell it to anyone.

## 4. Data storage

All App data — your settings, prayer times, bookmarks, reading position, and cached location — is stored **locally on your device** (using your device's private storage). We do not operate any servers and do not retain a copy of your data.

## 5. Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by **uninstalling the App** or clearing the App's data in your device's Settings. There is no account to delete.

## 6. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
